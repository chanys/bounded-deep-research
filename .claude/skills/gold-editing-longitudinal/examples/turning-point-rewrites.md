# Turning-point rewrites: three worked examples

Before/after for the turning-point rule, from the first gate review
(three questions run end-to-end). Use these to calibrate the
turn-vs-waypoint judgment. Do not copy their wording as templates;
every question's arc is its own.

## lc-0011 (agentic AI real-world capability, early 2025 - early 2026)

BEFORE (5 nuggets, score 0.5):
- n1 optimism WITH science/research/web-search/source-reliability specifics
- n2 tempering with 30% ceiling and benchmark-critique specifics
- n3 "revolution stalled" AND "SWE job-replacement narrative" (bundled)
- n4 RAG pre-trained-vs-retrieved reliability bottleneck
- n5 shift

AFTER (4 nuggets, same answer re-scores 1.0):
- n1 (start) optimistic about agentic capability and near-term impact [early 2025]
- n2 (turn) tempered: real-world performance weak on realistic evaluation [mid 2025]
- n3 (end) the anticipated revolution stalled [early 2026]
- n4 (shift) optimism -> tempering -> stalled [Feb 2025 - Jan 2026]

What moved and why:
- Specifics (science/research framing, 30% ceiling, SWE narrative)
  went into the milestones. They are one video's expression of a
  stance, not the stance.
- The RAG bottleneck failed the route-independence test: a correct
  answer can omit it and still tell this story. Waypoint -> milestone.
- The bundle in old-n3 was what forced the ad-hoc judge to invent
  half-credit. Split or trim bundles; verdicts stay binary.

## lc-0024 (authenticity of AI reasoning, Feb 2025 - early 2026)

BEFORE (5 nuggets, score 0.4): the mid-trajectory was sampled by two
specific stances - "fundamentally unsolved [Mar 2025]" and "map
language to formal logic [mid 2025]". The agent proved the same
descent with two DIFFERENT real stances - "fake reasoning chains
[Apr 2025]" and "illusion of a pattern machine [Jun 2025]" - and was
scored miss, miss.

AFTER (4 nuggets, same answer re-scores 1.0):
- n1 (start) treated reasoning as genuine but limited/improvable [early 2025]
- n2 (turn) grew doubtful displayed reasoning is genuine (fake/illusion) [spring-mid 2025]
- n3 (end) concluded reasoning is roleplayed, not genuinely implemented [late 2025 - early 2026]
- n4 (shift) genuine-capability framing -> doubt -> roleplayed [Feb 2025 - Jan 2026]

What moved and why:
- The turn is the nugget; WHICH mid-2025 skeptical stance proves it is
  the answer's choice. Old n2 became a milestone under the turn.
- The formal-logic proposal is off-axis: a method he proposed, while
  the question asks about his assessment of reasoning's state. Off-axis
  items are not nuggets at all.

## lc-0014 (AGI/superintelligence as a goal, mid 2025 - March 2026)

This one needed a human ruling first: the gold's single arc ("hardened
into dismissal") was one-sided; the corpus supports two threads held
together (dismissed AGI-as-marketing AND retained scientific AGI as a
distant research goal). The question span was also widened to March
2026 because a required stance rested on March 17 evidence.

AFTER (5 nuggets; the gate answer re-scores ~0.4 from 0.083, and its
remaining misses are genuine):
- n1 (start) skeptical that scaling delivers AGI [mid 2025]
- n2 (turn) dismissed AGI/superintelligence talk as largely marketing [early 2026]
- n3 (thread) still treated scientific AGI as a long-term research goal [late 2025 - early 2026]
- n4 (end) called the fragmentation into hard-coded skills a reversal away from emergent superintelligence [March 2026]
- n5 (shift) skepticism hardening into dismissal and reversal, while retaining the distant goal [mid 2025 - Mar 2026]

What moved and why:
- "AGI unnecessary given brute compute" and "social construct of
  agents": both are real stances, both omittable by a correct telling.
  Waypoints -> milestones.
- The two-thread shift is the lesson: when an answer disagrees with the
  gold arc and is grounded, the gold may be the one-sided party. That
  adjudication is a human ruling, never an automatic edit.
